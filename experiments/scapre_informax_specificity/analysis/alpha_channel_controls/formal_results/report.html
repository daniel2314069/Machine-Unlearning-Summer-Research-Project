Final-alpha channel controls

Does the exact correspondence between channels and final alpha values improve concept erasure? Under the registered official-empty-neutral configuration, the aggregate evidence says no.

Technical conclusion. Removing channel differentiation with constant_mean, or destroying channel identity while preserving the exact alpha multiset with shuffled, did not degrade any five-seed mean metric. Both controls instead produced small favorable movements—less than 0.4 percentage points. This is a narrow descriptive conclusion, not a claim that all assignments are universally equivalent.

Channel identity did not improve the established metrics

constant_mean changed the five-seed means by −0.083 pp Unlearn, +0.356 pp Preserve, and +0.334 pp Overall relative to official. shuffled changed them by −0.133 pp, +0.211 pp, and +0.217 pp. Both were favorable on Unlearn in 3/5 seeds and on Preserve/Overall in 4/5 seeds.

Aggregate effects are small

The control-versus-official changes are all below 0.4 percentage points. Across-seed sample standard deviations of the absolute metrics range from about 0.10 to 0.47 points. Each seed includes 1,200 target and 1,800 retain images: one changed target classification is 0.083 pp, and one changed retain classification is 0.056 pp.

B = I is viable, but not the best-performing control

identity_B narrowly improved all three official means: −0.033 pp Unlearn, +0.222 pp Preserve, and +0.205 pp Overall. But only 2/5 seeds favored it on Unlearn and 3/5 on Preserve/Overall. Against constant_mean, it was worse on all three means. Its per-matrix scale is also roughly 84–139 times the official mean alpha, so it should remain a paper-limit reference rather than replace repository behavior on this evidence.

Concept families move in different directions

The global means hide heterogeneity. For identity_B, target accuracy moved favorably for fruits, balls, and boats, but unfavorably for dogs and cats. Retain accuracy improved for cats and boats while declining for dogs, fruits, and balls. The mean result therefore does not justify a universal equivalence claim.

Scope, design, and definitions

The formal run uses Stable Diffusion v1.5, the registered 10 target and 15 similar retain concepts, the official empty-string neutral, five fixed edit seeds (20260820–20260824), and 120 images per concept. Each variant/seed therefore has 3,000 classifier rows.

  • Unlearn Accuracy: classifier accuracy over target images; lower is favorable.
  • Preserve Accuracy: classifier accuracy over retain images; higher is favorable.
  • Overall Accuracy: harmonic mean of forgetting (100 − Unlearn) and Preserve; higher is favorable.

The intervention changes only the final concept-max alpha vector before the closed-form edit. constant_mean preserves each matrix mean, shuffled preserves every exact multiset using a preregistered deterministic permutation, and identity_B sets all entries to one. The raw MI, pre-aggregate alpha, thresholds, non-alpha inputs, prompts, image seeds, sampler, CFG, generation count, and evaluator remain fixed.

Limits and next steps

The five fixed seeds are a deterministic robustness panel, not a random sample supporting population-level significance. Effects are small, classifier-based, and heterogeneous across families. The supported statement is only that channel-specific final-alpha assignment does not explain an aggregate advantage under this exact configuration.

  1. Treat the main channel-identity question as answered negatively for this configuration; do not spend GPU time on post hoc shuffle permutations.
  2. If scientifically valuable, preregister a family-focused replication around the observed dog/cat versus fruit/boat/ball split.
  3. Keep identity_B as a viable paper-limit reference, not a replacement default; a replacement would require an independently motivated scale study.
  4. Retrieve the formal cleanup sidecar to complete the server lifecycle record.
